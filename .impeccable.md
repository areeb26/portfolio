# Design Context

## Users
- **Primary**: Startup founders, agencies, businesses, content creators, tech recruiters
- **Context**: Evaluating Areeb Khan for AI automation projects or full-time roles
- **Job to be done**: Quickly assess competence, see real work, make contact decision

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Creative + Approachable + Sharp
- **Tone**: Confident but not arrogant, skilled but human, professional but not corporate
- **Feel**: "This person is talented AND enjoyable to work with"

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual style**: Editorial/Magazine - large typography, whitespace, unexpected layouts
- **Color**: Light theme with bold accent color (NOT dark mode AI slop)
- **Anti-references**: Generic developer portfolio templates, SaaS landing pages, glassmorphism/purple-blue gradients

## Design Principles
1. **Light and airy** - White/cream base, not dark mode. Feels fresh, not tired.
2. **Typography-led** - Headlines that make statements. Distinctive font choices.
3. **Editorial layouts** - Asymmetric grids, magazine-style compositions, break the predictable patterns
4. **One bold accent** - Pick ONE strong color and use it sparingly for impact
5. **Show personality** - Micro-copy, unexpected details, human touches that reveal character
6. **Motion with purpose** - Staggered reveals, scroll animations, but never gratuitous
